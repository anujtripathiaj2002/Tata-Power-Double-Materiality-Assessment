# Tata Power - Double Materiality Assessment

## Independent ESG Analytical Study | FY2025–26

An independent Double Materiality Assessment (DMA) of Tata Power based on publicly available information, designed to assess the materiality of key Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) topics from both impact and financial perspectives.

> **Disclaimer:** This is an independent analytical study based on publicly available information. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by Tata Power.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project was to independently assess Tata Power's ESG materiality landscape and benchmark the resulting assessment against Tata Power's publicly disclosed materiality assessment.

The study aims to:

1. Construct an independent DMA covering 30 ESG topics.
2. Assess topics across Impact Materiality and Financial Materiality.
3. Benchmark the independent assessment against Tata Power's official 27-topic assessment.
4. Identify areas of alignment and difference.
5. Compare the disclosure quality of both approaches.

---

## Methodology

The assessment follows a four-step analytical framework:

### 1. ESG Issue Universe
30 ESG topics were identified across:

- Environmental
- Social
- Governance

### 2. Impact Materiality Scoring

Impact scores were derived using:

**Impact Score = Average (Severity + Scope + Irremediability) × Likelihood Factor**

### 3. Financial Materiality Scoring

Financial materiality was assessed using:

**Financial Score = Financial Magnitude × Likelihood Factor**

Time horizon was also considered as contextual information.

### 4. Double Materiality Classification

A topic was classified as material when it reached the defined materiality threshold on either dimension.

Topics material on both dimensions were classified as **Double Material**.

---

## Key Findings

The independent assessment identified:

- **30** ESG topics assessed
- **22** Double Material topics
- **7** Impact Material only topics
- **1** Financially Material only topic

The highest-ranked topics included:

- Climate Change
- GHG Emissions
- Cybersecurity
- Energy Reliability
- Occupational Health & Safety

---

## Benchmark Results

The independent assessment was benchmarked against Tata Power's publicly disclosed materiality assessment.

Key comparison metrics:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Alignment Index | 85% |
| Exact Match Rate | 54% |
| Top-Band Recall | 60% |
| Top-Band Precision | 60% |

The comparison covered 26 topics with a direct official counterpart.

---

## Disclosure Quality Assessment

The project also compared the disclosure quality of the independent and official assessments using criteria informed by ESRS and GRI 3.

The assessment considered:

- Framework references
- Process disclosure
- Stakeholder engagement
- Scoring transparency
- Materiality thresholds
- Topic-level evidence
- IRO disclosure
- KPI linkage
- Assurance
- Refresh and approval
- Change log
